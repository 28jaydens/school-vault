2026-11-17 Other period Q2 · Hibiscus transpiration stats (FRQ). Students measured hibiscus transpiration rate in normal conditions vs a heat wave: table with 9 data points per column, mean and SD already given.
(a) Fill in the standard error for both and the 95% CI (mean ± 2 SE).
(b) Draw a box/bar plot with error bars for the two conditions.
(c) Write a conclusion for the experiment (do the 95% CI bars overlap? significant or not, link to stomata closing in heat).		 Accuracy, Precision, Standard Deviation and Standard Error · Lab 1 - Stomata Distribution and Statistics [ ]
2026-11-17 Other period Q3 · Biomolecule tests MCQ. Given results of 3 tests, pick the sample that has only glucose and protein, no starch. Answer: Benedict's turned red (reducing sugar), Biuret positive (purple, protein), IKI negative (no blue-black, no starch). Activity 13 - Biuret Test for Proteins · Activity 9 - Benedict Test for Reducing Sugars · Activity 8 - IKI Test for Starch [ ]
2026-11-17 Other period Q4 · C4 leaf structure. Given a C4 leaf cross-section: identify the tissue where RuBisCO is made (bundle sheath cells) and where maltase is made (mesophyll, where starch is broken down), name the circular structure around each vein (bundle sheath / Kranz anatomy), and state which environments favour the C4 adaptation (hot, dry, high light, where stomata close and O₂ builds up). C3, C4 and CAM Pathways · Activity 12 - Leaf Structure, Dicot and Monocot · Leaf Tissues and Cell Types · Enzymes and RuBisCO [ ]
2026-11-17 Other period Q5 · Light intensity graph. Two curves of photosynthesis rate vs light intensity: one at 0.4% CO₂, one at 0.04% CO₂.
(a) Limiting factor at the end of the curve (plateau: CO₂ concentration, or temperature).
(b) Limiting factor at the beginning (light intensity).
(c) Calculate the percent difference between the two plateaus.
(d) Explain why percent difference is used instead of raw difference (compares relative size, removes units/scale).		 Factors Affecting Photosynthesis and Limiting Factors [ ]
2026-11-17 Other period Q6 · Photorespiration FRQ. A passage about some scientists whose point is photorespiration. Explain why photorespiration is bad for the plant (RuBisCO binds O₂ instead of CO₂, no sugar made, wastes ATP and fixed carbon, releases CO₂). Enzymes and RuBisCO · C3, C4 and CAM Pathways [ ]
2026-11-17 Other period Q7 · Electrophoresis and chromatography. Given a gel electrophoresis result:
(a) Find which lanes have the most similar protein (matching band positions).
(b) Given a chromatogram, calculate the Rf value (distance pigment travelled ÷ distance solvent travelled).
(c) Explain why electrophoresis is better than chromatography (separates by size and charge, higher resolution, many samples side by side, works for proteins/DNA not just pigments).		 Gel Electrophoresis · Lab 2B - Gel Electrophoresis of Dyes · Paper Chromatography and Rf · Lab 2A - Pigment Chromatography [ ]

Topics that keep coming up#

  • ### Question formats to expect

  • Circle and name functional groups on a molecule model (Q1)

  • Statistics FRQ: SE and 95% CI calculation, plot with error bars, written conclusion (Q2)
  • MCQ reading results of the three biomolecule tests (Q3)
  • Label tissues on a leaf cross-section and name the structure (Q4)
  • Read a two-line graph for limiting factors, then a percent difference calculation (Q5)
  • Short explanation FRQ from a passage (Q6)
  • Interpret a gel, calculate Rf, compare the two methods (Q7)
