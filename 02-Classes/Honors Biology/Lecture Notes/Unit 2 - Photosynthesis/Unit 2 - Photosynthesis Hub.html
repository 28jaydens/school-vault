How the ideas connect

flowchart TD
    SUN([Sunlight]) --> LEAF[Light hits the leaf:<br/>reflected, transmitted, absorbed]
    LEAF --> PIG[Pigments absorb<br/>blue-violet and red]
    PIG --> EXC[Chlorophyll electron excited]
    EXC --> PS[Photosystems II and I]
    PS --> LR[Light reactions<br/>thylakoid membrane]
    H2O([H₂O]) --> LR
    LR --> O2([O₂])
    LR -->|ATP + NADPH| CAL[Calvin–Benson cycle<br/>stroma]
    CAL -->|ADP + NADP⁺| LR
    CO2([CO₂ in through stomata]) --> CAL
    CAL --> GLU[Glucose]
    GLU --> STA[Starch: storage]
    GLU --> CEL[Cellulose: cell wall]
    GLU --> RESP[Cellular respiration → ATP]
    GLU --> FC[Food chain:<br/>producers → consumers]
    FC --> PYR[Only ~10% passes<br/>up each level]
    RESP --> HEAT([Heat lost])
    PYR --> HEAT
    STOM[Stomata and guard cells] --> CO2
    STOM --> TRANS([Water lost: transpiration])
    STA -. tested with .-> IKI["IKI: blue-black"]
    GLU -. tested with .-> BEN["Benedict's: blue to orange"]

Five threads that run through the whole unit

  1. Energy flows one way and is lost as heat at every step. Sun → leaf (a few percent kept) → producer → consumer (10% kept). Second law of thermodynamics. Energy Efficiency of Photosynthesis → Energy Pyramid and the 10 Percent Rule
  2. Excited electrons are the currency of photosynthesis. Catch them and you get ATP and NADPH; lose them and you get heat and red light. Chlorophyll Excitation and Fluorescence → Photosystems → Z Scheme
  3. Structure fits function. Stacked thylakoids, an H⁺-tight membrane, chloroplasts at the cell edge, a helix that traps iodine, guard cells that bend. Chloroplast Structure, Carbohydrates and Glycosidic Bonds, Stomata and Transpiration
  4. Photosynthesis and respiration are a loop. One stores energy in glucose, the other releases it; the products of each are the reactants of the other. Photosynthesis Overview ↔ Glycolysis and Cellular Respiration
  5. Every gain has a cost. Open stomata gain CO₂ and lose water. Stomata and Transpiration, Photorespiration, C4 and CAM Plants

Links to earlier units

  • Characteristics of life: metabolism (energy exchange), organization (organelle → cell → tissue → organ → ecosystem), homeostasis, response to stimulus.
  • Stimulus & positive negative feedback: guard cells closing when water is short is negative feedback.
  • Orgins of LIfe: biomolecules (carbohydrates), phospholipid membranes, ATP as energy.
